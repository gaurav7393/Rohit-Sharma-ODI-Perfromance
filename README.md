# Rohit Sharma – ODI Career Performance Analysis (2007–2019)

## Project Title
**Rohit Sharma ODI Batting Performance Analysis Dashboard**

## 🎯 Objective
To analyze Rohit Sharma's One Day International (ODI) batting career using innings-by-innings data (2007–2019), clean and structure the raw scorecard data, and build an interactive Excel dashboard and supporting report that surface trends in:
- Year-on-year run-scoring and batting average
- Performance against different oppositions
- Ground-wise / venue-wise scoring
- Milestone conversion (fifties and hundreds)
- His best-ever ODI innings (264 vs Sri Lanka)

The goal is to turn raw statistics into a clear, decision-ready visual story that anyone — analyst or fan — can explore in seconds.

## 📊 Dataset
- **Source:** ESPNcricinfo Statsguru (ODI batting innings list)
- **Records:** 206 innings entries (202 innings batted, 4 "Did Not Bat")
- **Period:** 23 June 2007 – 2019
- **Fields:** Runs, Balls Faced, Strike Rate, 4s, 6s, Opposition, Ground, Date, Match ID

## 🔑 Highlighted Key Insights
- **8,010 career runs** in 202 innings at an **average of 46.84** and a **strike rate of 87.95**.
- **22 centuries** and **41 half-centuries** — roughly 1 in every 3 innings crosses fifty.
- **Highest score: 264** vs Sri Lanka at Kolkata (13 Nov 2014) — the **highest individual score in ODI history**, off 173 balls (33x4, 9x6, SR 152.60).
- Holds **three of the four highest individual ODI scores ever** (264, 209, 208*) — a feat no other batsman has matched.
- **2013–2018 was his peak stretch**, contributing ~68% of his total career runs, with **2017 his best year** (1,293 runs at an average of 61.6).
- **Most prolific against Australia** (1,980 runs at 55.0 average) and **Sri Lanka** (1,562 runs) — these two oppositions alone account for over 44% of his career runs.
- Strong scoring spread across both home venues (Mohali, Kanpur, Bengaluru) and overseas grounds (Melbourne, Sydney, Dubai), showing consistency across conditions.

## ✅ Conclusion
This project demonstrates a complete data-analytics workflow — from raw, messy scorecard exports to a cleaned dataset, PivotTable-driven summaries, an interactive Excel dashboard, and a fully documented report. The analysis confirms Rohit Sharma's place among the greatest ODI batsmen of his era, driven by an elite scoring average, an unmatched record of double centuries, and consistent performance across years, oppositions, and venues. The dashboard and report together make these insights easy to explore and communicate.
